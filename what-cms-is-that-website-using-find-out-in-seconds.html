What CMS Is That Website Using? Find Out in Seconds

You land on a beautiful website and wonder: how was this built? Is it WordPress, Shopify, Wix, or something custom? Knowing the answer is useful in more ways than most people realize. Web designers study competitors' platforms to choose the right stack for their next project. Marketers check whether a prospect's site runs on a platform they support. Security researchers identify outdated systems. Curious bloggers simply want to know what powers the sites they admire.

Why Knowing a Site's CMS Matters

A CMS detector answers that question instantly. It analyzes a website's code signatures, meta tags, script patterns, and other fingerprints to identify the content management system behind it, along with the technology stack. Enter any URL and within seconds you know whether the site runs WordPress, Shopify, Joomla, Drupal, Squarespace, or one of hundreds of other platforms.

A Practical Tool for Freelancers and Agencies

For freelancers and agencies, this is a practical sales tool. If you build WordPress sites, finding prospects already on WordPress means easier migrations and plugin compatibility. If you specialize in Shopify, spotting Shopify stores tells you exactly who to approach. Instead of cold outreach to everyone, you target sites on platforms you know best.

Planning Redesigns and Migrations

For site owners planning a redesign, checking competitors' platforms reveals industry trends. If every top site in your niche runs on the same CMS, that platform probably has the plugins, themes, and community support your niche needs. If a competitor's site is fast and yours is slow, their stack might be part of the reason.

Developers use detectors during audits and migrations. Before moving a site to a new platform, you need to know what you are moving from: its plugins, its structure, and its dependencies. A quick detection saves hours of manual code inspection.
